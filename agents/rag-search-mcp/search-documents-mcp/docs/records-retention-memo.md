# Riverside Family Clinic — Records Retention Update (MOCK — fictional)

**Memo effective:** 2026-01-01
**Supersedes:** prior retention schedule in the patient policy document.

## Updated retention periods
- Adult medical records are now retained for **8 years** after the last date of
  service. This replaces the previous 7-year period.
- Records for patients who were minors at the time of service are retained until
  the patient's 25th birthday.
- Diagnostic imaging studies are retained for 10 years from the date of the
  study.

## Reason for change
The retention period for adult records was extended by one year to align with an
updated regional requirement that took effect at the start of 2026.

## Unchanged
Procedures for requesting records are unchanged: requests may be made in writing
or through the patient portal, and copies are provided within 30 days.
