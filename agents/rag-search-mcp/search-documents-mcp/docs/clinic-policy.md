# Riverside Family Clinic — Patient Policies (MOCK — fictional clinic)

## Appointment cancellations
Please give at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule an
appointment. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice, or missed
appointments where the patient does not attend, may incur a 25 dollar late
cancellation fee. Fees are waived for documented emergencies.

## Prescription refills
Allow 48 hours for routine prescription refill requests. Refills for controlled
medications cannot be processed by phone and require an appointment. Request
refills through the patient portal or by calling the pharmacy line.

## Medical records requests
Patients may request a copy of their medical records in writing or through the
patient portal. Records are provided within 30 days of the request. The clinic
retains medical records for 7 years after the last date of service for adult
patients, in line with regional retention requirements.

## Telehealth visits
Telehealth video appointments are available for follow-ups, medication reviews,
and minor concerns. New patient visits and any complaint requiring a physical
examination must be seen in person. A working camera and microphone are required,
and the patient must be physically located within the region at the time of the
visit.

## Billing and insurance
Co-payments are due at the time of service. The clinic will submit claims to
participating insurers on the patient's behalf. Patients are responsible for any
balance not covered by their plan.
